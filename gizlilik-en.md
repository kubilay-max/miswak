# Miswak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This document explains what data the Miswak app processes and when it
sends data to external services.

## In short

Miswak does not ask you to create an account. Your prayer records, dhikr
counters, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device; they are
not sent to any server of ours. Some features need to contact third-party
services in order to work, and each of those is listed below.

## Location

Your location is needed to calculate prayer times and the direction of
the Qibla. These calculations run on your device, and your location is
not sent anywhere for them.

The following features send your coordinates to the service they rely on:

**Finding your city name.** Your device's own geocoding service is used
to show the name of the place you are in. On iOS this is Apple's service,
on Android it is Google's.

**Weather.** Your coordinates are sent to Open Meteo
(api.open-meteo.com).

**City search.** When you search for a city by hand, your search text is
sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service
(nominatim.openstreetmap.org).

**Nearby mosques and halal places.** Your coordinates are sent to the
OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de,
overpass.kumi.systems).

If you do not grant location permission, the app asks you to choose your
city by hand; the location-based features above then work according to
the city you chose.

## Quran, hadith and audio content

Quran texts, translations and hadith texts are downloaded from open
source archives (cdn.jsdelivr.net, cdn.islamic.network). These requests
contain only which content is to be downloaded; no personal data is
sent.

Adhan recordings are downloaded the same way.

## Map applications

When you ask for directions to a mosque, the map application you chose
opens (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Yandex). This opens a link on your
device; the app does not send data to those services on its own.

## Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob advertisements. AdMob may collect
data such as device identifiers in order to serve and measure ads. See
Google's privacy policy for details. The Pro version shows no
advertisements.

## Purchases

Pro membership purchases are handled through RevenueCat together with the
Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details are not passed to
the app.

## Notifications

Prayer time notifications are scheduled on your device. Notification
content is prepared inside the app; it is not sent from a server.

## Backup

When you create a backup, your data is written to a file and you decide
where to save it. The app does not upload your backup anywhere.

## Children

Miswak is not an application designed specifically for or targeted at
children. Miswak does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. Any significant change will be announced
inside the app.
